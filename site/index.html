EVERYTHING ON ONE SCREEN

ApexNorth connects to your PlayStation over Wi-Fi and turns GT7's telemetry stream into something you can race with.

Drive

Shift lights, gear and RPM, live delta to your best lap, tyre temperatures, fuel per lap and your pit window, at 60 Hz.

Record

Every lap saved at 30 Hz with five sector splits. Tracks and cars are named automatically: 121 layouts, 584 cars.

Analyse

Replay any lap on the track map and race a ghost of your other laps in the same car, with synced speed, throttle, brake and gear.

Listen

A built-in offline voice engineer calls lap times, sectors, fuel, the pit window, tyres and race position. Pick Michael, Heart, George or 19 more.

Strategy

Fuel burn, stops needed, earliest, target and latest pit lap, and estimated tyre fade, combined into one pit plan.

No PlayStation?

Try demo mode: a simulated 10-lap race with a pit stop, tyre wear and a spin, so you can see everything first.

AFTER THE FLAG

Every session goes into your Activity feed. Dig into any lap to find where the time went.

  • Which Mac? Apple menu → About This Mac. "Chip: Apple M…" is Apple Silicon, "Processor: Intel" is Intel.
  • Mac, first launch: ApexNorth isn't registered with Apple yet, so open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. The one-command install skips this.
  • Windows: if "Windows protected your PC" appears, click More info → Run anyway. Allow ApexNorth through the firewall so it can hear your PlayStation.
  • Want the newest features early? The beta build has them, and may have bugs. All releases.

ON TRACK IN A MINUTE

Your computer and your PS5 or PS4 just need to be on the same network.

  1. Get on track in GT7. A race, time trial, lobby or practice. The game doesn't send telemetry from menus.
  2. Open ApexNorth and click Find my PlayStation. Or type its IP from Settings → Network on the console.
  3. Drive. Laps record themselves and your engineer is on the radio.
